Rules & what’s being measured

Action probabilities and critic values are reconstructed from each selected checkpoint. They are not the optimizer’s original training-buffer values. A high probability is a preference, not a win probability.

The bootstrap calculation shows what PPO would do at this checkpoint. It does not by itself establish why the policy loops.
